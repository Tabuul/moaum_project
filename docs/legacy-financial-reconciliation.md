# Legacy financial reconciliation (V625)

*21 October 2026.* **Bursary → Students Account → Legacy Financial Reconciliation** (`/finance/legacy-reconciliation`; the Bursar
has it under Finance as well, Audit reads it). The University's brief: a controlled road for the old portal's financial records —
outstanding fees and e-wallet top-ups — from the Students Account Officer's confirmation, through the Bursar's approval, to the
student's central wallet, with nothing credited before the Bursar approves and nothing credited twice. This note is the final report
the brief asked for: what existed, what was reused, what was added.

## 2. What V625 adds

**The desk.** One screen with five views: *Overview* (the figures — student liabilities and student credits never combined, the
requests by state, the old e-wallet balances by state), *Outstanding fees* (a session, student by student), *E-wallet top-ups*
(the V583 desk's figures, the desk a click away), *Wallet credit requests* (the queue: pending the Bursar, returned) and *History*
(credited, rejected, withdrawn; the e-wallet postings). Each view prints to Excel and PDF with S/N first. The officer's dashboard
and the Bursar's carry the figures.

**Outstanding fees reconciliation** (`finance.legacy_outstanding_rows(session, state, q, fac, dept, limit, legacy_only)`): every
student of the session with old-portal school-fees payments, with the fees due by the fee setup, what was paid (old portal semester
one, two, whole session; the retired Payment History upload; the portal), the doubles found, and the classification:

| Classification | Meaning | What may happen |
|---|---|---|
| **OWES** (student owes the University) | paid less than the fees stated | nothing: a legacy outstanding fee is a liability of the student and is never wallet money — a credit request for the student is refused (`LEGACY_CREDIT_NOT_OVERPAID`) |
| **CREDIT** (credit held for the student) | paid more than the fees stated, not yet decided | the officer confirms it and requests the wallet credit; or the Bursar carries or refunds it on Old Fees History |
| **CREDIT_DECIDED** | carried, refunded or credited to the wallet (decision shown) | nothing more: one decision stands |
| **SETTLED** / **NO_FEE_STATED** | paid what is stated / the Bursary has not stated the session's fees | — / the Bursary states the fees first |

**The legacy credit request** (`finance.legacy_credit_request`, reference `LOR/YYYY/NNNNNN` from its own sequence; the id a UUID):

1. **The officer confirms and initiates** (`finance.legacy_credit_initiate`; Students Account Officer or Bursar). The window shows
   the student, the legacy record (fees due, paid, paid on the old portal, the excess, the doubles, the decision standing), the
   old-portal payments behind the figures, every session's position, the wallet balance; and asks the confirmed amount (never above
   the excess the records show: `LEGACY_CREDIT_AMOUNT`), the evidence (`LEGACY_CREDIT_EVIDENCE`), the reason
   (`LEGACY_CREDIT_REASON`), the reason for a different amount (`LEGACY_CREDIT_ADJUSTMENT_REASON`; the request is then marked
   **adjusted** for the Bursar) and the explicit confirmation (`LEGACY_CREDIT_CONFIRM`; the API refuses a blind submission). Refused
   where the overpayment is already decided (`LEGACY_CREDIT_SETTLED`), where the Bursary's bank check for a double charge still holds
   the student (`LEGACY_CREDIT_DOUBLE_CHARGE_CHECK`, V561), where a request already stands (`LEGACY_CREDIT_OPEN`: one open request a
   student and session, a unique index), for a merged or deceased record. **Nothing is credited.** The Bursar's office holders are
   told (`LEGACY_CREDIT_WAITING`).
2. **The Bursar decides** (`finance.legacy_credit_decide`; a Bursar officer other than the initiator: `LEGACY_CREDIT_SAME_OFFICER`
   and the check constraint `ck_lcr_two_officers`). *Approve*: the overpayment and the wallet are locked, the request must still be
   pending, the overpayment must still be undecided and the excess still cover the confirmed amount (read afresh:
   `LEGACY_CREDIT_EXCESS_CHANGED`), the bank check must not hold the student; then one `CREDIT` on `finance.wallet_entry` (funding
   source **OVERPAID**, the student's own money, reference the request's, the note carrying the figures, the officer, the evidence
   and the adjustment), one `overpayment_decision` **WALLET** (so the excess can never also be carried or refunded: `overpayment_settled`
   reads it), the request CREDITED; the student told (`LEGACY_CREDIT_POSTED`: the amount, the session, the reference, the balance),
   the officer told (`LEGACY_CREDIT_DECIDED`). All in one transaction: a refusal leaves no partial credit. *Reject* and *Return for
   correction* need a note of ten characters or more; the officer is told, and the student of a rejection (`LEGACY_CREDIT_REJECTED`,
   without the desk's note).
3. **Returned, the officer corrects and resubmits** (`finance.legacy_credit_resubmit`: the excess read afresh, the same rules as the
   first submission, the Bursar told again) or **withdraws** (`finance.legacy_credit_withdraw`, with a reason).
4. **Never twice.** A credited request is closed (`LEGACY_CREDIT_STATE`); the wallet entry is referenced once (`wallet_entry_id`
   unique); a second approval, or `decide_overpayments` carrying the same excess, finds the decision standing.
5. **The student uses it** on the wallet page exactly as any wallet money — applied to a session's fees wholly or in part
   (`finance.apply_wallet`), the shortfall through the gateway, never below zero, every application a wallet debit and a confirmed
   school-fees payment with its receipt, on the statement with the balance.

**The ledger.** An `OVERPAID` credit is drawn from the school-fees income of the overpaid session (`gl_wallet_credit_account` →
`gl_income_account('School fees')`, the session on the entry) into **2100 Student deposits & wallet**: the cash was booked when the
old payment was imported, so no new cash and no revenue counted twice — the overpaid income becomes a wallet liability, as V506's
CARRY moves it between sessions. Applying the credit to fees posts Dr 2100 / Cr fee income, as for every wallet application.

**The old e-wallet (V583) gains:** the Bursar's **Return for correction** (`legacy_wallet_decide('RETURN')`: a verified balance,
by an officer other than the verifier; status RETURNED, verified again by the officer after the correction), the verification
confirmed in a window that shows each balance's original top-ups, what was used, the eligible balance and the amount to credit with
the explicit confirmation and the remarks (never one click), and the notices: the Bursar told of balances verified and waiting
(`LEGACY_WALLET_WAITING`), the verifier told of a rejection, freeze or return (`LEGACY_WALLET_DECIDED`), the student told of a
rejection (`LEGACY_WALLET_REJECTED`).

**The full record** (Student 360, Fees and payments) lists the student's legacy credit requests beside the old e-wallet.

## 3. Who may

| Office | May |
|---|---|
| Students Account Officer, Bursar | read the desk; confirm and initiate, resubmit, withdraw a request; verify and enter statements on the e-wallet desk |
| Bursar | approve, reject or return a request (never one they initiated); post, return, reject, freeze, reopen an e-wallet balance (never one they verified) |
| Audit, Deputy Audit, Registry, ICT, admin, super | read |

The API names the offices (`LegacyReconciliationController`: READERS, INITIATORS, DECIDERS) and the database holds every rule
(`LEGACY_CREDIT_OFFICE`, the two-officer check, the unique open request, the unique wallet entry, the advisory locks). A student's
reach is checked on every student-level read and write (`StudentReach`). Nothing from the screen is trusted: the amount is checked
against the excess the server reads, the student against the request, the approval against the office and the initiator.

## 4. The API (`/api/v1/finance/legacy-reconciliation`)

`GET /figures?session`, `GET /sessions`, `GET /outstanding?session&state&q&fac&dept&limit&legacyOnly`, `GET /requests?status&session&q&fac&dept&limit`,
`GET /requests/{id}`, `GET /students/{id}`, `POST /requests` `{student, session, amount?, reason, evidence, adjustmentReason?, confirmed}`,
`POST /requests/{id}/resubmit`, `POST /requests/{id}/withdraw` `{reason}`, `POST /requests/{id}/decide` `{action: APPROVE|REJECT|RETURN, note}`.
The e-wallet desk's `POST /legacy-wallet/{id}/decide` takes `RETURN` as well.

## 6. What needs the Bursary's hand

- Fees must be stated for a legacy session before its rows classify (NO_FEE_STATED otherwise; 2024/2025 had none on the live copy
  of 16 October).
- Doubles on the old records are withdrawn on Old Fees History; a payment under the wrong student is moved by a payment swap; both
  before a credit is confirmed.
- The old e-wallet balances held for review (more paid from the old wallet than the top-ups on file) wait for the old portal's
  statement to be entered.
- The suspense the old-portal credits are drawn from is settled on Accounting → Wallet opening (V552); the OVERPAID credits need
  nothing there.
