# Paid Post-UTME applicants report (V479)

Admissions → **Paid Post-UTME Applicants** (`/admissions/paid-applicants`, route `t/paidapplicants`) lists the candidates of an
admission session whose Post-UTME application fee is paid, and downloads them with **DOWNLOAD PAID POST-UTME APPLICANTS** as an
Excel workbook or a PDF.

## Who is listed

The rules live once, in `admissions.post_utme_paid_applicants(session)`; the list, the figures and every download read it.

- **Post-UTME applicant** — an application of the session on the applicant stack that is not CCE's nor a Preliminary
  programme's: UTME and Direct Entry (and Transfer) candidates.
- **Paid (VERIFIED)** — an `APPLICATION` fee reference the payment service confirmed (`admissions.fee_reference.confirmed_at`,
  set by the gateway's verified notification or the Bursary's confirmation). Nothing the browser says counts.
  - **REVERSED** — the Bursary reversed the payment (V420 clears the confirmation and keeps `admissions.fee_reversal`).
  - **PENDING** — references opened, none confirmed (failed, abandoned, expired or awaiting the gateway).
  - **NOT_STARTED** — no application-fee reference at all.
  - A checking, acceptance or school fee never makes an applicant paid.
- **One row an application** — applications are unique per candidate and per account; several confirmed attempts show the first
  payment and count the attempts (`verified_payments`).

The report opens on VERIFIED. The other statuses are there for reconciliation; their downloads are titled
"POST-UTME APPLICANTS — <status>", never "PAID".

## The columns

S/N · JAMB Registration Number · Candidate's Full Name (SURNAME, Other names) · Course/Programme Applied For · Gender ·
UTME Aggregate Score · Phone Number · Email Address.

- **UTME aggregate** — JAMB's, from the CAPS record of the candidate's session (as the merit list reads it, V384), else the
  aggregate stored on the candidate. Never computed or substituted. A Direct Entry candidate reads
  "NOT AVAILABLE (DIRECT ENTRY)".
- **Gender** — the candidate's, else JAMB's on the same CAPS record.
- **Programme** — the candidate's programme, matched to the register by name for the faculty and department filters.
- **Contacts** — the applicant account's. The placeholders the old portal's applicants came over with (V168:
  `<jamb>@migrate.moau.local`, `00000000000`) are not contacts and read as missing.

A value the record does not hold reads **NOT AVAILABLE** on the screen, in Excel and in the PDF; the row is never dropped.
The *Data check* panel counts what the selection is missing (JAMB number, programme not on the register, gender, phone,
e-mail, UTME aggregate). On the live-shaped copy of 13 Oct 2026, 2026/2027 held 12,649 paid applications (12,298 UTME, 351
Direct Entry); only 298 carried a real phone number and 2 a real e-mail — the rest came from the old portal without contacts.

## Filters, figures and scope

Admission session (the intake session by default), payment status, faculty → department → programme (searchable, each list
holding only what the session's applicants applied for), gender, entry mode, payment date range (Lagos days), JAMB number and a
search by name, JAMB number, application number or programme — every one a bound parameter on the server. The figures (in all,
by gender, by entry mode, by faculty, by programme, by payment status) are counted from the same rows and apply themselves
when chosen.

| Office | Reach |
|---|---|
| Academic Office, Registry (Registrar, Deputy Registrar), Directorate of ICT, admin, super | The whole University |
| Dean, Faculty Officer | Their faculty |
| Head of Department | Their department |

The bound is `OfficeScope.bound` on the server: a department office asking for another department or its programme is
refused, for the list and the download alike. The portal's permissions are offices; `PAID_VIEWERS` stands for
VIEW_PAID_POST_UTME_APPLICANTS and `PAID_EXPORTERS` for EXPORT_PAID_POST_UTME_APPLICANTS_EXCEL / _PDF.

## Downloads

`POST /api/v1/admissions/sessions/{session}/paid-applicants/export` returns the whole filtered selection, names A–Z, and records
the download in `admissions.report_export` (report, session, format, filters, row count, who, office, when — on the audit
spine). Excel is built in the browser by `brandedXlsx` (the University's logo and name, the title, the session, the filters,
generated date and time, generated by, the total; frozen, filterable header; S/N numeric from 1; JAMB and phone numbers as text).
The PDF is built on the server by the document engine (`/admissions/paid-applicants/pdf`, BROADSHEET profile, landscape: the
header repeated on every page, S/N running on, "Page X of Y" and the generation line in the footer). The button reads
PREPARING REPORT… / GENERATING EXCEL… / GENERATING PDF… while it works and takes no second click.

## API

| Endpoint | |
|---|---|
| `GET …/paid-applicants?status&fac&dept&prog&sex&entry&from&to&jamb&q&page&size` | a page (≤ 200) with the total and the missing-data counts |
| `GET …/paid-applicants/summary?…` | the figures |
| `GET …/paid-applicants/options` | sessions with applications; faculties, departments and programmes within the scope |
| `POST …/paid-applicants/export` `{format: XLSX\|PDF, …filters}` | every row, and the download recorded |

On the live-shaped copy each read of the function takes 60–200 ms for the 12,649 applications.
