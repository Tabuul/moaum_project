## 1 Introduction and about the portal

### 1.1 What the portal is

The MOAUM Unified University Portal is the single web application of Rev. Fr. Moses Orshio Adasu University, Makurdi (formerly Benue State University). It holds the University's registers — applicants, students, staff, courses, fees, results, accommodation, documents — and lets each person act on the part of the register that is theirs. A student registers courses, pays fees, reads results and asks for a transcript on it; a lecturer enters marks on it; a Head of Department approves registrations and allocates teaching on it; the Registry matriculates on it.

Three principles show through every screen, and it helps to know them:

- **Nothing is typed beside the record.** Totals, grades, GPA, fee charges, class lists and score sheets are computed from the register. A lecturer types two numbers (CA and examination); the portal does the rest. A student never types a course code; the courses offered to their programme are listed for them.
- **Every change is attributed.** Each write carries the acting person, the acting office and a reason, and is kept on a tamper-evident audit trail. That is why every action button asks you to be signed in as one office, and why some actions ask for a reason in words.
- **A gate is a rule, not an opinion.** Course registration opens when the semester's school fees are paid in full; a score sheet leaves the lecturer only when every registered candidate carries a mark or an outcome; a result reaches the student only on a Senate minute. When the portal refuses you, it says which rule refused you and, usually, which office can help.

### 1.2 Who uses it

| Kind of person | Signs in with | Home screen |
|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate applicant | JAMB registration number (or the `APP/YY/NNNNNN` application number) | `/applicant` — My application |
| Postgraduate applicant | Email address or `PG/YY/NNNNNN` application number | `/pg/portal` — Dashboard |
| Student (undergraduate) | Matriculation number, or the admission number `MOAUM/ADM/YY/NNNNNN` before matriculation | `/student` — Dashboard |
| Postgraduate student | Matriculation or admission number | `/student` — Dashboard (postgraduate menu) |
| College of Health Sciences student (200 Level and above) | Matriculation or admission number | `/college/student` — the College dashboard |
| Lecturer, Head of Department, Dean, Examinations Officer and every other member of staff with an office | Staff number (username) or the email address the Registry set as username | `/` — the office's own dashboard |
| External examiner | Email address (it becomes the username on activation) | `/examiner` — Examiner workspace |

A member of staff is on the portal only after the Registry (or the Directorate of ICT) has created the person, granted at least one office and issued a first password. Students are on it from the moment the Academic Office brings them onto the register; a student's applicant password carries over on first sign-in.

### 1.3 One sign-in door

Everybody signs in at the same page, `/login`. You type your number or email address and your password; the portal reads the shape of what you typed and sends you to the right side of the house. Below the form are links for the two public application doors ("Post UTME Registration" → `/apply`, "Postgraduate application" → `/pg/apply`) and "Verify a payment or receipt" (→ `/verify`).

> **Note:** A "Sign in with the University's single sign-on" link appears only when single sign-on is configured on the server. It is **not deployed**; you will not see it, and you sign in with your password.

### 1.4 Browsers and devices

The portal is a website; nothing is installed. Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop, a tablet or a phone. The screens are built to work at phone width: below about 900 pixels the sidebar becomes a drawer opened with the menu button, and below about 760 pixels tables scroll sideways inside their panels, tiles stack two to a row and buttons grow to a finger-sized height. PDFs (course form, receipt, examination card, statement of results, identity card, documents) open in a new tab and can be saved or printed from the browser.

### 1.5 How this manual is written

- Screens are named by their menu label and their address in the browser, for example **Course Registration** `/student/register`. Navigation paths are shown as indented lists:

  ```text
  Academic
   → Course Registration
  ```

- Messages the portal shows are quoted exactly as it shows them, in quotation marks.
- Offices are named by their titles with the portal's code in backticks on first mention, for example Head of Department (`hod`).
- Where a feature is unfinished, a call-out says so: **Planned / Not Yet Implemented**, **Placeholder** or **Partially implemented**. A feature described without such a call-out is implemented and in use.
- `> **Screenshot Required:**` markers show where the printed edition carries a picture of the screen.

## 2 Getting started

### 2.1 Signing in

> **Screenshot Required:** Sign in — `/login` — the two-column sign-in page with the crest panel on the left and the card with "Your number or email address", "Password" and "Sign in" on the right.

1. Open the portal address in your browser. You land on **Sign in** `/login`.
2. In **Your number or email address**, type the identifier for your kind of account (see the table in §1.2). As you type, a hint under the box tells you who the number looks like ("A student, on the matriculation number", "An applicant, on the JAMB number", and so on).
3. Type your **Password**. The eye button shows or hides it.
4. Press **Sign in**. The button is disabled until both boxes are filled.
5. You are taken to your home screen. If the Registry set your password (a new staff account, or a student account the Registry opened by hand), you are sent first to **Your password** `/account/password` (staff) or **Profile** `/student/profile?change=1` (student) and must choose a password of your own before anything else opens.

What you will see if it goes wrong:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "That username and password do not match an account." | Staff: unknown username, wrong password, or the person's record has been ended. The same words are used whichever it is. |
| "That number and password do not match a student account." | Student: wrong number or password, or the student has no admission or matriculation number yet. |
| "That number and password do not match an application account." | Applicant: wrong JAMB/application number or password. |
| "No portal account has been opened for this number yet." | A student who never had an applicant account (a migrated student). The Registry opens the account from the student's record. A migrated account may sign in the first time with the student's own number as the password. |
| "This account is locked after repeated failures; try again after HH:MM." | Five wrong passwords in a row lock the account for fifteen minutes. |
| "The portal was updated. Sign in again." | The service was redeployed; every earlier session is refused by design. Sign in again. |

> **Tip:** The hint under the identifier box uses an older pattern for matriculation numbers. A number in the current format (for example `MOAU/AD/ACC/26/13568`) may be labelled "A member of staff, on the staff number" while you type it. Ignore the label; the portal still signs you in as a student.

> **Note:** A demonstration roster of invented accounts exists for training (`docs/demo-accounts.md` in the repository). Demo accounts sign in as `demo.<office>` for staff and `MOAUM/MTC/26/9901` and neighbours for students. The password is in that file and is not printed here.

### 2.2 Forgotten password and reset

1. On the sign-in page click **Forgot your password?** → `/login/forgot`.
2. Type your staff number, matriculation number, application number, email or JAMB number and press **Send the reset link**.
3. The page always answers "If that names an account, a reset link is on its way" — whether or not an account matched. This is deliberate.
4. If an account matched **and** it has an email address or phone number on record, an email "Reset your MOAUM password" and an SMS are sent with a link to `/login/reset?token=…`. The link works for **one hour** and **once**.
5. Open the link. On **Choose a new password** type the **New password** and **Type it again**, then **Save the new password**. Eight characters at least; "The two do not match." appears inline if they differ. Success: "Your password has been changed". An incomplete link says "This link is incomplete"; a spent or old one, "This reset link has expired or was already used."

> **Warning:** Delivery depends on an email or SMS provider being configured by the Directorate of ICT and on your account carrying an email or phone. A staff account with no email on record is emailed only if its username is itself an email address. If nothing arrives, ask the Registry to reset your password from **Users & roles** (it also clears a lockout), or a student can ask the Registry from the Student record.

### 2.3 Changing your password

- **Staff**: **Your password** `/account/password` (you are sent there automatically when the Registry set your password). Fields **Current password**, **New password**, **New password, again**; button **Change the password**. The new password must be at least **ten** characters and must not contain your username.
- **Students**: **Profile** `/student/profile`, the **Current/New password** fields; at least **eight** characters.
- **Applicants**: through the forgotten-password link only.

Nothing is emailed when a password is changed.

### 2.4 Several offices: the office switcher

A member of staff may hold more than one office (a lecturer who is also Head of Department, for instance). The sidebar's **Signed in as** box lists every office your account carries. Choose one and the menu, the dashboard and your rights change to that office's. The choice is remembered in your browser.

- An office granted to you today appears in the list at your **next sign-in**, not before. Sign out and in.
- If a grant has been ended since you signed in, the portal answers "The office 'x' is not one this token carries" — sign out and in.
- Every action is recorded against the office you were acting as at the time, so choose the right office before you approve, sign or enter marks.

### 2.5 Signing out, session limits and lockout

- **Sign out** is the button at the foot of the sidebar. It ends the session on the server and clears the browser's cookies.
- A session lasts **12 hours** from sign-in; after that every request answers "This session reached its end. Sign in again."
- Whenever the portal is **redeployed**, every session opened before the deployment is refused: "The portal was updated. Sign in again."
- **Five failed sign-ins** lock the account for **fifteen minutes** (staff, students, applicants and postgraduate applicants alike). Postgraduate applicants are additionally slowed after twenty failures from one connection in fifteen minutes.
- Nothing is sent when you sign in, sign out or are locked out.

### 2.6 Where notifications are

The portal writes the same notice to your email address, your phone (SMS, where the event sends one) and to a page inside the portal:

| Who | Where inside the portal |
|---|---|
| Student | **Account → Notifications** `/student/notifications`, and the "Notices sent to you" panel on the dashboard (the last 30). Each row shows the channel and a state — *Sent*, *Waiting to be sent* or *Not delivered*. |
| Lecturer and other staff | **Overview → Notifications** `/me/notices` (in the lecturer's menu; any staff account may open the address). Tiles All / This week / By email / Not delivered; filters by search and channel; each notice expands to its text. |
| Undergraduate applicant | "Notices sent to you" on **Overview** `/applicant`. |
| Postgraduate applicant | The "History of this application" list on `/pg/portal` shows the trail; the notices themselves go to your email. |

Section 7 lists every event that sends a notice, and — equally important — the events that send **nothing**.

### 2.7 How the screen is organised

> **Screenshot Required:** A staff dashboard — `/` — the Shell with the sidebar (crest, "Signed in as" select, folded menu groups with badges, sign-out at the foot), the top bar (breadcrumb, title, identity chip, "Search records") and the content column with tiles and panels.

**The sidebar** (dark blue, on the left) carries the crest, the **Signed in as** office select, the menu groups and, at the foot, **Sign out**. Groups fold and unfold; a number badge beside a group or an item is a live count of what waits there (registrations to approve, transcripts in the queue, and so on). A collapse control shrinks the sidebar to icons on a wide screen; on a phone it becomes a drawer behind the menu button.

**Menu groups by kind of account:**

- Staff offices draw from the set **Overview · Academic · Students · Admissions · Finance · Staff · Services · Reports · Administration · Me**. Each office sees only the groups and items that concern it (a lecturer sees Overview, Academic, Students, History and Me; a Dean sees Overview, Academic, Students, Admissions, Finance, Reports and Me).
- Students see **Start here · Academic · Learning · Services · Account**.
- Postgraduate students see **Overview · Academic · Finance · Research · Documents · Communication · Graduation · Profile**.
- Undergraduate applicants see **My application · Screening · Admission**.
- External examiners see one group, **External Examiner**.

**The top bar** shows the menu button (phone), a breadcrumb (group › item), the page title and subtitle, your name and office (the identity chip) and, for staff, a **Search records** button (keyboard shortcut `/`) that opens **Search** `/search` over students, staff, courses and credentials. Every search for a person is recorded against your account.

**Inside a page** you will meet the same building blocks everywhere: a *note* (a coloured box that states the one thing waiting on you), *tiles* (large figures), *panels* with tables, a *role line* ("You may act — Head of Department" or "Signed in as Lecturer · view only") that tells you whether your office may press the buttons, and *toasts* (brief messages at the corner of the screen: "Saved", or the refusal's title). A refusal also renders a red **problem notice** with the rule, the detail, "What to do" and the office that can help.

Every data table has a search box once it holds more than eight rows ("Search these N rows…"), pages of 10/25/50/100/All, a footer count and a **Print** button that prints the table headed with the University's name and the date.

> **Note:** A few menu items still point at screens that exist only in the design prototype. Clicking one shows "<label> is still the prototype's screen". They are named in this manual where they occur.

## 3 Student manual

This section walks through every screen a student has. Read §3.1–3.6 first: they are the order of a session — dashboard, fees, registration, matriculation. Postgraduate students and College of Health Sciences students have different menus for some of these tasks; see §3.23 and §3.24.

### 3.1 The dashboard

> **Screenshot Required:** Dashboard — `/student` — the fee-gate note, the identity card with passport, "This session" steps, quick tiles and "Notices sent to you".

```text
Start here
 → Dashboard
```

**Dashboard** `/student` reads your record and shows, from the top:

1. A **standing banner** if the published results have pronounced you *on probation* or *advised to withdraw* (see §3.9).
2. The **fee gate** note: "You are cleared to register" when this semester's school fees are settled, otherwise "Action required" with **Pay now** and **See breakdown**. If the Bursary has not yet stated what a payment releases for the session, the note reads "What a payment releases is not yet stated for this session".
3. Your **identity card** preview with your passport photograph.
4. **This session** — three steps: On the register → Course registration → Examination docket, each ticked when done.
5. A read-only **Student details** card (name, numbers, programme, level, status).
6. **Quick tiles**: My results, Fees & payments, Graduation (final year) or My documents, Deferment, Hostel and **Course form** — the last is enabled only once your registration is approved.
7. A **carryover warning** naming the courses you owe.
8. **Notices sent to you** — the last 30 messages the portal sent you, with channel and state.

### 3.2 Profile, password and biodata

```text
Account
 → Profile
 → Biodata
```

**Profile** `/student/profile` shows the Registry's fields read-only and lets you keep three things current:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Phone | Nigerian mobile number: eleven digits beginning with 0. `+234 803…` and `803…` forms are normalised. Refusal: "A Nigerian mobile number is eleven digits beginning with a zero." |
| Personal email | Must be a valid address. This is where password resets and notices go. |
| Contact address | Free text. |

Below them, **Current password** / **New password** with a show/hide button; the button is enabled once the new password is eight characters or more. When you arrive with `?change=1` the page says "Choose your own password before you go on".

> **Note:** The "On the register since" line on this screen always shows "—". It is a known display fault, not a gap in your record.

**Biodata** `/student/biodata` is the long form the University keeps on you, in sections: Identity (read-only), Personal, Contact, Origin & sponsorship, Parents or guardian, Next of kin & guarantor, Health, Bank account, Documents and Change history. Each field carries a badge:

- **From JAMB** — locked; it came from your admission record and is changed only by the Registry.
- Open fields save at once when you press **Save** or **Save and continue →**.

> **Placeholder:** Some fields are badged "Needs approval" and the Registry has a *Biodata Changes* queue for them. In the current configuration no field is set to that tier, so nothing you type ever enters that queue; open fields save directly and locked fields are refused with "That field is locked". The screen text saying "the student is notified" of a decision describes a path that sends nothing.

### 3.3 School fees and payments

> **Screenshot Required:** Fees & payments — `/student/fees` — the three tiles (Session charge, Paid, Outstanding), the status note, "The charge" table and the "Pay" panel with a reference and the card/USSD button.

```text
Start here
 → School Fees — Pay First
```

**How a charge arises.** The Bursary states a fee schedule per session. Your charge is computed from it for your level, entry mode, faculty, programme, indigene status and the semesters that are open; you never type an amount, and nobody can invoice you for a line that is not on the schedule. Charges accumulate as semesters open. A charge of "—" with "No charge is stated for {session} yet" means the Bursary has not stated the schedule for your level or programme.

**What a payment releases.** Course registration for a semester opens when that semester's school fees are paid **in full**. The examination card, results, transcript and convocation are released under the Bursary's *clearance scheme* (the seeded scheme releases registration, the identity card and the library at the first instalment and everything else on payment in full; hostel is never gated). Arrears from an earlier session block everything while they stand.

**Procedure — pay school fees**

1. Open **School Fees — Pay First** `/student/fees`. Read the tiles: **Session charge**, **Paid** ("Settled in full", "Instalment 1 of 2" or "Nothing yet") and **Outstanding**.
2. In the **Pay** panel choose what to pay: **First semester · ₦x**, **Full session · both semesters · ₦x**, or **Second semester · ₦x** (only the choices that apply are shown).
3. Press **Generate a reference for ₦{amount}**. The portal mints a payment reference of the form `MOAUM-FEE-…` valid for **24 hours** and shows it in large type: "Quote this reference and nothing else: at a bank branch, by transfer, or by card below."
4. Pay against the reference in one of three ways:
   - **Card or USSD**: press **Pay ₦x by card or USSD**. If more than one gateway is live you choose Paystack, Flutterwave, Quickteller or Quickteller PayDirect. Card gateways take you to the gateway's own page and bring you back to this screen; the portal confirms the payment the moment the gateway tells it the money landed.
   - **Quickteller PayDirect**: the screen shows "Pay ₦x to {biller} on Quickteller" with your PRN (the reference), a pay link, a USSD code and the biller code for a bank branch or ATM. After paying press **I've paid — check now**. "Not confirmed yet" means the collection has not reached the University; wait a few minutes and check again.
   - **Bank branch or transfer**: quote the reference. The Bursary confirms it against the bank's record.
5. When confirmed, the reference appears in **Payment History** as **Paid** with a **Receipt** link, and you receive "Your payment is confirmed" by email and SMS (receipt number included). Until then it is **Awaiting confirmation**; after 24 hours unconfirmed it is **Expired** — generate a new one; a reference is free.

Refusals you may meet:

| Message | Cause and remedy |
|---|---|
| "no charge is stated for {session} yet" | No schedule for your level/programme. Ask the Bursary. |
| "the amount X is more than the balance of Y" | Pay the balance or part of it; nothing is taken beyond what is owed. |
| "This reference has expired." | Generate a new one. |
| "This reference is already confirmed as paid." | Nothing more to pay on it. |
| "A card checkout needs an email address on your record" | Add a personal email under Profile, or pay by PayDirect or at a bank. |
| "Card and USSD payment arrive when a payment gateway is wired to the portal." | No gateway is live yet; pay at a bank against the reference. |
| "The checkout could not be opened (…)" | Try again shortly, or pay by transfer or at a branch against the reference. |

**Receipt.** **Payment receipt** `/student/receipt/{reference}` shows the receipt (number `RCT-YYYY-NNNNN`, date, payer, matriculation number, programme, session, semester, purpose, amount, channel, gateway or teller reference) with a QR code and a **check code**. **Download PDF** produces the one-page official receipt. Anyone — a bank, an office, a parent — can check it at `/verify/receipt/{reference}?c={code}` or by scanning the QR; the page answers "Genuine — this receipt is on the Bursary's ledger" or "Not verified". Before confirmation the screen says "This payment is not confirmed yet" and the PDF answers "Not confirmed".

> **Note:** The subtitle of the Fees screen reads "2026/2027 session" whatever session you are looking at; it is fixed text. The "Instalment 1 of 2" caption assumes two instalments.

### 3.4 Wallet and funding (NELFUND, scholarships, top-ups)

```text
Services
 → Wallet & Funding
```

**Wallet & funding** `/student/wallet` is the ledger of money the University holds on your behalf: NELFUND loan remittances, scholarships and grants the Bursary credits, and your own top-ups. You do not apply for NELFUND here — the Fund's decision is recorded and shown ("The Fund approved your NELFUND loan", "…did not approve…" with the reason, or "…is still with the Fund").

| Task | Steps |
|---|---|
| Apply the wallet to fees | When "Your wallet covers what you owe" shows, press **Apply ₦x to {session}**. The portal mints a school-fee reference for the amount and confirms it at once (channel "NELFUND wallet"); the receipt appears on the Fees page and the "Your payment is confirmed" notice is sent. |
| Top up | Under **Top up the wallet** type an **Amount** and press **Generate the reference**; pay it by card/USSD from the same panel or on the Fees page. When confirmed, the wallet is credited. |
| Withdraw a surplus | Under **Withdraw to your bank account** enter **Amount** (blank = all), **Bank**, **Account number**, **Account name** and press **Request the withdrawal**. Allowed only when the session's fees are cleared in full, there are no arrears, the wallet has a balance and no withdrawal is pending; otherwise the panel says "Not available yet" and why. The Bursary approves and a second officer records the payout; states REQUESTED → APPROVED → PAID, or REJECTED with the reason. |

### 3.6 The matriculation number

**What it looks like now.** Since the Registry's schedule was configured (V263), a matriculation number is built as

```text
MOAU / {FACULTY} / {PROGRAMME} / {YY} / {SEQUENCE}
```

- `MOAU` is the University code.
- `{FACULTY}` is the faculty's segment (for example AD for Management Sciences, BM for Basic Medical Sciences, PHRM for Pharmaceutical Sciences, LAW for Law, TS for Technology, CS for Communication).
- `{PROGRAMME}` appears **only** where the programme is configured to carry a code (for example `ACC`, `CMP`, `MTH`). Medicine and Surgery, Pharmacy, Law and every postgraduate programme carry none, and the separator is simply omitted — there is never an empty segment. MBBS carries its own faculty segment `MBBS`.
- `{YY}` is the last two digits of your session of entry.
- `{SEQUENCE}` is drawn from a named series the faculty or programme belongs to (Administration, College, Pharmacy, Architecture, General); it only ever moves forward and a number already used is never reused.

Examples of the shape: `MOAU/AD/ACC/26/13567`, `MOAU/MBBS/26/6095`, `MOAU/PHRM/26/199`, `MOAU/LAW/26/85632`. Numbers issued before the change (the `MOAUM/DEPT/YY/NNNN` shape, and older `BSU/…` numbers) remain valid and remain yours.

**When it is issued.** Matriculation turns an ADMITTED student into an ACTIVE one. A number is issued when, and only when:

1. your school fees for the session of entry are **paid in full**;
2. your course registration for that session is **approved** by your Head of Department;
3. your name is on the **faculty list** the Faculty Officer confirms to the Academic Office (a queried name is held back until the query is withdrawn); and
4. the Academic Office or the Registry **runs matriculation** for the session — every faculty's list in one act — or issues a single number to a straggler who paid and registered later.

**What happens next.** You are told by email and SMS — "Your matriculation number is X. It is permanent, it opens the portal in place of your admission number…". From then on you sign in with the matriculation number; the admission number is retired (never deleted, never reused). The number is permanent: the register refuses any attempt to change it ("the matriculation number … is permanent and is not changed"). A deferment, an identity card, a transcript or a certificate can be asked for only once you are matriculated.

> **Note:** The applicant-side **Matriculation** page (`/applicant/matric`) still describes the older `MOAUM / dept / YY / NNNN` format in its "What you will be issued" table. The number you receive follows the format above.

### 3.13 Deferment

> **Screenshot Required:** Deferment — `/student/deferment` — the state banner, the Deferment application fee panel (Pay ₦10,000 Deferment Fee / the receipt), the three-step form, the approval timeline, the Academic effect panel and the Deferred courses table.

```text
Academic
 → Deferment
```

A matriculated student may apply to defer one semester or a whole session. The **application fee** (₦10,000 unless the Bursary states otherwise) is paid first; the form opens only when the payment is confirmed. The application then passes, in this order, the **Bursary** (which verifies your last school-fee payment from the finance record), your **Head of Department**, your **Faculty**, the **Academic Office** (which forwards approved applications to the DVC in a numbered batch), and the **Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic)**, whose approval, given with a comment, is final.

**Eligibility.** The banner says "DEFERMENT APPLICATION NOT AVAILABLE" with the reason when: you are not yet matriculated; your status is graduated, withdrawn, expelled, transferred out, deceased, rusticated or suspended; an application is already live; or you have used the sessions the University allows (2 in all; a semester counts a half).

**Procedure**

1. Open **Deferment** `/student/deferment`. In **Deferment application fee** press **Pay ₦10,000 Deferment Fee**. A payment reference is generated (valid 24 hours). Pay by card or USSD with **Pay now**, or at any bank branch quoting the reference (the Bursary confirms it). The panel then shows Amount, Payment Status PAID, Transaction Reference, Payment Date and **View Receipt** / **Download Receipt**. The fee is not refunded if the application is refused.
2. The form opens. Step 1: your **Student information** is read from the record (you cannot change it). Choose **Deferment type**, **Academic session**, **Semester**, **Reason** and write the **Additional explanation** where the reason needs it. **Save and Continue**.
3. Step 2: **Supporting documents** (PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB, at most six). A medical, maternity or employment reason requires one. You can open a document you uploaded in a viewer on the page.
4. Step 3: **Review**, tick the declaration and press **Submit Deferment Application**. Toast: "Deferment request DEF-… submitted successfully". The application is now **WAITING BURSARY ACTION**.
5. Follow the **Deferment application** panel: Application number, Application fee, Payment status, Application status, Current office, and the **Approval timeline** — Application submitted ✓ → Bursary approved → Head of Department approved → Faculty approved → Forwarded to the DVC by the Academic Office → Deputy Vice-Chancellor approved (final approval). The status badges read WAITING BURSARY ACTION, WAITING HOD ACTION, WAITING FACULTY ACTION, WAITING ACADEMIC OFFICE ACTION, WAITING DVC ACTION, APPROVED, APPROVED · IN FORCE, COMPLETED, REJECTED, RETURNED FOR CORRECTION.
6. If a desk **returns** the application, the banner names the office, the date and the reason; press **Continue the Application**, correct it and submit again — it goes back to the same desk, and the history is kept. If a desk **rejects** it, the reason is on the record and the application is kept. While in review you may **Withdraw Application**.
7. When the Deputy Vice-Chancellor approves, you receive "DEFERMENT APPROVED" by email and SMS with the deferred period, the duration, the expected return, the number of courses affected and the timeline extension. The daily clock brings the period into force on its start date: your status becomes DEFERRED and registration for that period is refused. Fourteen days before the return date you are reminded; on your return the department, faculty or Registry confirms it.

**What the approval does to your record** (the **Academic effect of your deferment** panel): the courses of the deferred period are marked **DEFERRED** — no F, no zero, no carry-over, no GPA or CGPA change; your **expected completion** moves by exactly one semester (or one session); your **entry session** and **matriculation number** never change. The **Deferred courses** table lists each course with its original period and its status — DEFERRED, REGISTERED (once you take it again) or COMPLETED (once a result is published). After your return, they appear on your registration form under **Deferred / catch-up courses**, added automatically like carry-overs but never called failed; their grades enter your GPA only when you complete them.

You are told by email and SMS at every turn (§7). **Download Approval Letter** opens the letter once the DVC has approved (§8).

**Matriculation and your sign-in (V267).** Until you are matriculated you sign in with your admission number. When the Registry issues your matriculation number you are told by email and SMS; from that moment the matriculation number is your portal sign-in username — same account, same password — and the admission number no longer opens the portal. The number is permanent and appears on every document the University issues to you.

### 4.1 Undergraduate applicants: the ten stages

A candidate whom JAMB has admitted to the University registers with the JAMB registration number alone. Name, programme, UTME score and O'Level results are read from the list JAMB sent the University and are never typed. One account then carries you through ten stages that the portal computes; the **Overview** `/applicant` shows a rail of them with "what next".

| Stage | What it means | Who completes it |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Account created; application fee not confirmed | you (registration) |
| 1 | Application fee confirmed | the gateway or the Bursary |
| 2 | Application form submitted | you |
| 3 | Seated in a published screening batch | the Academic Office |
| 4 | Screening score released | the Academic Office |

#### Stage 0 — Register by JAMB number

1. Open **Post UTME Registration** `/apply` (link "Post UTME Registration" on the sign-in page). The session is the current one unless the link names another.
2. Type your **JAMB registration number** (twelve digits then two or three letters). As soon as it is complete the portal looks it up:
   - "Found on the UTME/Direct Entry list JAMB sent the University" — your **Surname and other names** and **Programme you chose** appear read-only, and the remaining fields open.
   - "That number is not on the list JAMB sent the University" — the number is wrong, you did not choose this University on CAPS, or the tranche is not yet loaded. Do not travel to the campus to resolve this.
   - "Nobody can be verified yet, and so nobody is let through" — the University has not loaded JAMB's list yet.
   - "The University is not admitting into {programme} this session".
   - "An application account already exists for this number" — sign in instead.
3. Fill **Email address** (a typo hint such as "Did you mean gmail.com?" may appear), **Phone number** (eleven digits beginning with 0; +234 forms are accepted — "A Nigerian mobile number is eleven digits beginning with a zero."), **Choose a password** and **Confirm password** (eight characters at least — "Eight characters at the very least.").
4. Press **Continue**. Your account is created with an application number `APP/YY/NNNNNN`, you are signed in and taken to **Overview** `/applicant`. You receive "Your MOAUM applicant account" by email and SMS. An email already used by another account is refused ("the address … already belongs to an application account").

Afterwards you sign in at `/login` with the JAMB number (or the application number) and this password. Forgotten password: the sign-in page's link; the reset link lasts one hour.

#### Stage 1 — Application fee

**Application Fee** `/applicant/fee` shows the **Post-UTME screening fee** plus the **Portal and payment charge** and the **Total payable** (the Bursary sets them per session; the defaults are ₦2,000 and ₦300).

1. Press **Generate a reference for ₦…**. The reference (`MOAUM-APP-NNNNNN-XXXX`) is valid for 24 hours; **Generate a new reference** replaces an expired one.
2. Press **Pay ₦… by card or USSD** and pay on the gateway, or pay by bank transfer or at a branch quoting the reference (the **How you can pay** table). For PayDirect use **I've paid — check now** after paying.
3. When confirmed the page shows "Payment confirmed — ₦… received" with a **Receipt** panel, and you receive "Your payment receipt · RCT-…" by email and SMS. The form now opens.

#### Stage 2 — Application form

**Application Form** `/applicant/apply` is closed until the fee is confirmed ("The form opens when your application fee is confirmed"). Then three tabs:

1. **1 Biodata** — read-only from JAMB (name, date of birth, sex, state and LGA, JAMB number, UTME score or "Direct Entry"). The one field you type is **Next of kin — name and phone**; it saves when you leave the box and **cannot be changed after submission**.
2. **2 O'Level results** — your sittings exactly as JAMB sent them, or "No O'Level result has reached the University from JAMB yet". You do not enter O'Level results yourself.
3. **3 Review and submit** — a gate list ("Next of kin is missing" until you give it), the declaration "I declare that the particulars I have given are true…", **Save and come back later** and **Submit application**. Refusals: "the form opens when the application fee is confirmed"; "the next of kin is not given"; the declaration must be ticked.

Submitted: "Your application was submitted on …", the **What you submitted** panel and **Print / Download (PDF)** of the application form. No notice is sent on submission.

> **Note:** No document is uploaded at this stage. There is **no upload control** on any applicant screen: your passport photograph comes from JAMB's download, and originals are checked in person at clearance (stage 7). (An upload endpoint exists in the API but nothing on the screens calls it.)

#### Stage 3 — Screening slip and CBT seating

**Screening Result** `/applicant/score` reads "Scores are released when every batch has been screened" until release; then tiles **UTME** (scaled), **Post-UTME screening** (its source — CBT, examination or O'Level computation), **Aggregate** (green or red against the cut-off) and **Departmental cut-off**, with **How your aggregate was calculated** and **Where you stand** (merit position, places). "No cut-off is stated for your programme yet" if none is set. You are told "Your screening result is released". A remark on a score is made at the Registry within seven days. The O'Level screening score itself is never shown to applicants.

#### Stage 5 — Admission decision

**Programme eligibility** (V266) appears on the Overview and on Admission Status as soon as the form is submitted. The portal reads your record — the O'Level result as JAMB sent it, your UTME subjects and score (or the Direct Entry award the Office captured) — against the session's admission settings for the programme you applied for, and shows one of four verdicts: **ELIGIBLE**, **ACADEMICALLY ELIGIBLE — ADDITIONAL SCREENING REQUIRED** (the programme also requires a named screening), **NOT ELIGIBLE**, or **PENDING VERIFICATION** (a result the rules need is not yet on your record). Nothing changes until the Office approves; you are told either way, and an approval is not an offer of admission — the Board decides in the normal way. You cannot request a programme the engine did not list, a second request while one is open, or any change once the Board's decision is released. **Recalculate** re-reads your record at any time; the reading also refreshes by itself when the settings or your record change, and the panel names the policy version it was read under.

> **Screenshot Required:** Programme eligibility — `/applicant/status` after submission — the verdict panel with its reasons and the "Programmes you may be eligible for" table with View Details and Request Change.

- "You are on the waiting list";
- "You were not offered a place this session" (with the Board's note).

> **Note:** When the University offers places through JAMB's own admission-status list, the decision is released without a notice. Check the screen. A waiting-list place is not promoted automatically; the office decides.

**Accept Your Offer** `/applicant/accept` opens only on a released offer. "To accept, you must do both":

1. Read the **Undertaking**, tick "I have read the undertaking and I accept it." and press **Sign the undertaking**.
2. **Generate a reference for ₦…** (the acceptance fee plus any checking fee; ₦25,000 by default if the Bursary has not set it — it is **non-refundable**) and pay by card/USSD, transfer or branch, as for the application fee.

When both stand, the offer is accepted: "Offer accepted — ₦… received", a **Receipt** with "Non-refundable: Yes", and the notice "Your place is held". **Print Offer Letter** now appears on Admission Status → the admission letter PDF (§8). #### Stage 7 — Document clearance

**Document Clearance** `/applicant/clearance` opens after acceptance ("Clearance opens when you have accepted your offer"). It lists six originals the Registry checks **in person**: O'Level certificate or statement of result; Birth certificate or declaration of age; Local government identification; JAMB admission letter; Medical fitness certificate; Passport photographs (six copies). > **Partially implemented:** The Registry records each item through the API; no officer screen for it was found in the current build, so this stage may show "Not presented" for longer than the physical clearance took. It does not block stages 8 and 9.

#### Stages 8 and 9 — Becoming a student

Once the Academic Office brings the session's admitted candidates onto the register you receive an **admission number** `MOAUM/ADM/YY/NNNNNN` (shown on **Matriculation** `/applicant/matric` under **What you carry now**; "Not issued yet." before). ### 5.4 Score sheets

> **Screenshot Required:** Score entry — `/results/sheets/{id}` — the header pill, the Download the template / Upload a completed sheet / Save the draft / Submit and attest buttons, and the roll table with CA, Exam, Total, Grade, Points and Outcome columns.


**Where a sheet comes from.** The Academic Office or Exams & Records opens an **examination session** (Main, Re-sit or Special) for a session and semester with dates and a score-sheets-due date. Opening it generates one score sheet per offering that already has a lead lecturer; allocating a lecturer while the session is open creates the sheet at once. A Re-sit sheet carries only the candidates who were absent or scored 0 points on the published Main sheet; a Special sheet the absentees.

**Score Sheets** `/results/sheets` lists your sheets for the session and semester: course, candidates, entered ("N scripts held" if any), stage pill ("N days overdue", "Returned once"), second examiner ("Not yet set"), and **Continue** / **View**. "A sheet that has left this desk is readable, not editable."

**Procedure — enter and submit marks** (`/results/sheets/{id}`)

1. **Download the template**: an Excel workbook with every registered candidate already on it, in alphabetical order — columns S/N, Matriculation number, Name, Programme, Level, CA (0–{caMax}), Exam (0–{examMax}), Outcome — with data validation on the mark columns. The split (CA out of 40 and examination out of 60 by default) is the course's own setting; the tiles show "CA out of".
2. Either type the marks on the screen — **CA** and **Exam** per row (Enter, ↓ and ↑ move down and up a column; a cell over its maximum turns red with "More than N") — or fill the workbook and **Upload a completed sheet** (.xlsx or .csv). An upload is checked whole before anything is written: if any line is refused (unknown outcome, out of range, a locked row changed) the whole file is refused — "{file} was not accepted — N lines refused, nothing written" — with **Download Validation Report**. Candidates on the upload who are not on the roll are set aside: **Hold these N scripts** (§5.6) or **Leave them**.
3. For a candidate who did not sit or must not be graded, set the **Outcome**: Graded (the default when both marks are typed), Absent, Withheld, Incomplete, Malpractice or Exempted. A blank is not an outcome.
4. **Save the draft** as often as you like. The rule on saving: both CA and exam or neither; each within its maximum ("{code} assesses out of 40 and examines out of 60; a mark outside that was entered."). Once saved, a mark is **locked** — "A saved mark is on the record; the lecturer does not change it on their own." — until the sheet is returned to you.
5. When every row carries a mark or an outcome the header reads "Complete — not yet submitted". Press **Submit and attest** → **I attest these marks**. Total, grade and points are computed by the register (a raw total of 39 is graced to 40); you never type them.

Refusals: "N registered candidate(s) on this sheet have no mark and no outcome — … A blank is not an outcome."; "The sheet is at {stage}; a mark changes by amendment with a reason, not by entry."; "An amended mark carries its reason."

**Returned sheets.** If a later desk returns the sheet, the note reads "Returned to you once/N times" quoting the office and its comment, and "Returned to you — every mark is open for amendment". Change what must change, give the **Reason, if amended** on each changed row, and submit and attest again. Every change is a new version on the record; nothing is overwritten.

**Exports.** **Marked sheet · Excel / · PDF** (once anything is entered): the roll with CA, exam, total, grade, points and outcome and a performance summary (grade counts, mean, highest, lowest). The PDF is the University's landscape A4 print; "The record is the register, not this print."

**Score Sheet History** `/results/sheets/history`: tiles Sheets on record / Published / In approval / Still with you; filter by session, semester and standing; Excel/PDF of each marked sheet.

### 5.5 What happens after you attest

Attesting moves the sheet from **Entry** through a chain of desks; you can follow it on the sheet ("This sheet has left the lecturer: {stage}" → **Open the approval chain**) and on your dashboard's **Track Approval**:

```text
ENTRY (you)
 → VERIFICATION           — Examinations Officer
 → DEPT_BOARD             — Head of Department
 → FACULTY_SCRUTINY       — Faculty Examinations Officer
 → FACULTY_COMPILATION    — Faculty Officer
 → FACULTY_BOARD          — Dean
 → RECORDS                — Exams & Records
 → SENATE                 — Registrar / Deputy Registrar (Academic Affairs), on a Senate minute
 → PUBLISHED              — visible to students
```

Rules that bind the chain: every candidate must carry a mark or an outcome before the sheet leaves you; the same person may not take two consecutive stages ("you approved the previous stage of this sheet; another desk must approve this one"); a result is published only on a cited Senate minute. Any desk may **return** the sheet to you with a reason; it re-enters at verification when you resubmit. A sheet whose fail rate exceeds half its candidates is flagged for review at each desk (a caution, not a block).

> **Warning:** Nothing is emailed to students when a result is published, and nothing is emailed to you when a sheet is returned — the dashboard's Notifications panel text suggests otherwise, but no such notice is generated today. Look at the sheet's stage. The **Remind** / escalate buttons an officer may press on an overdue sheet also send nothing ("The notification module is not on the portal yet; nothing was sent.").

> **Placeholder:** On a published sheet the approval chain shows **Raise an amendment** and **View as a student**; neither button does anything. A published sheet cannot be reopened or amended on the portal.

### 5.6 Held scripts

Under every score sheet is the panel **Scripts from candidates not on the roll**. A candidate who sat the paper without being registered for the course is held by matriculation number, not graded:

1. Type the **Matriculation number**, the **Outcome**, **CA**, **Exam** and an optional **Note** (e.g. "script no. 47, sat in LT 2") and press **Hold the script** — or **Download the held-scripts template**, fill it and **Upload held scripts** (accepted whole or not at all).
2. The student is told at once by email and SMS ("Your {code} script is held until you register").
3. When the student's registration for the course is approved, the mark is **released** into your sheet as a new version, automatically. If the semester's late-registration date passes first it **lapses** ("Late registration for this semester closes on {date}; a script not released by then lapses and never grades."). You may **Withdraw** a script while it is still held.

Refusals: "X is on the roll of {code}; enter the mark on the sheet"; "no student on the register is numbered X"; "a script is already held for X on {code}"; "late registration for this semester closed on {date}…".

## 9 Troubleshooting

Each entry gives the message you see, the real cause and what to do.

### 9.1 Cannot sign in

| You see | Cause | Do |
|---|---|---|
| "That username and password do not match an account." | Wrong staff number/email or password; or the Registry has ended your person record. | Check the number; use **Forgot your password?**; if nothing arrives, ask the Registry to reset it from Users & roles. |
| "That number and password do not match a student account." | Wrong matriculation/admission number or password. Before matriculation sign in with the **admission** number; after it, with the **matriculation** number. Applicants who have just been admitted use the same password as their applicant account. | Use **Forgot your password?** (email or phone on record needed). |
| "No portal account has been opened for this number yet." | A migrated student with no applicant account behind them. | The Registry opens the account from the Student record (needs a matriculation number); a migrated account may accept your own number as the first password. |
| "This account is locked after repeated failures; try again after HH:MM." | Five wrong passwords. | Wait fifteen minutes, or ask the Registry to reset (which clears the lock). |
| "The portal was updated. Sign in again." / "This session reached its end. Sign in again." | A deployment, or twelve hours passed. | Sign in again. |
| "The office 'x' is not one this token carries" | Your office grant ended after you signed in. | Sign out and in. |

## 10 Frequently asked questions

1. **Which number do I sign in with?** Staff: your staff number (or the email the Registry set as username). Students: the admission number `MOAUM/ADM/YY/NNNNNN` until you are matriculated, then the matriculation number. Undergraduate applicants: the JAMB registration number. Postgraduate applicants: the email address or `PG/YY/NNNNNN`. External examiners: the email address.
2. **I was an applicant; do I need a new password as a student?** No. Your applicant password carries over on your first sign-in as a student. A postgraduate applicant's password carries over too.
3. **How long am I signed in?** Twelve hours, or until the portal is redeployed, whichever comes first.
4. **Why does the sign-in page say I look like a member of staff when I type my matriculation number?** The hint uses an older number pattern. It is cosmetic; you are still signed in as a student.
5. **Where do I see what the portal sent me?** Students: Account → Notifications. Staff: `/me/notices`. Applicants: the dashboard's "Notices sent to you".
6. **Why did I not get an email when my results were published / my registration was approved?** Because the portal sends nothing on those events. Check the screen. Section 7.2 lists every silent event.
7. **How is my fee worked out?** From the Bursary's fee schedule for your level, entry mode, faculty, programme and indigene status, as the semesters open. Nobody types an amount against you.
8. **Can I pay part of the fee?** Yes — a semester at a time (the first semester releases first-semester registration). The examination, results and transcript wait on the session paid in full under the current scheme.
9. **My payment reference expired. Did I lose money?** No. A reference is a number, not a payment. Generate a new one; if you paid at a bank against the old one, the Bursary confirms it against the bank's record.
10. **Can I choose more units than the maximum with my HOD's permission?** Not on the portal. The hint mentions an overload approval, but no such mechanism exists; keep within the range.
11. **What does "Returned to you" on my registration mean?** Your Head of Department sent it back with the reason shown. Change what is asked and submit again.
12. **When will I get my matriculation number?** After your fees for the entry session are paid in full, your registration is approved, your faculty's list is confirmed and the Academic Office runs matriculation. You are emailed and texted the number. It never changes.
13. **What do MOAU/AD/ACC/26/13567 and MOAU/LAW/26/85632 mean?** University code / faculty segment / programme code (only where the programme carries one) / year of entry / sequence in the faculty's or programme's series.
14. **What is the difference between the statement of results, the broadsheet and a transcript?** The statement is one semester's published results with a QR (from Results). The broadsheet is your whole history for your own use, no QR. A transcript is an official document requested under My Documents, numbered, verifiable by code, and released by the Registry (full and sessional transcripts are paid; the mini-transcript and statement of academic record are free and instant).
15. **The Results screen has an "Official transcript" button that does nothing.** It is a stale placeholder. Use Academic → My Documents.
16. **How long do I have to query a mark?** Seven calendar days from publication, one mark in one course per query. The answer comes by email and SMS. A "Corrected" answer does not by itself change the published mark on the portal.
17. **Why can't I see my course space?** Your registration is not approved yet, or the lecturer has not published anything.
18. **Who approves a deferment and how long does it take?** Bursary → HOD → Faculty → Academic Office → DVC (final). You are told at each turn; once approved, a daily clock brings it into force on its start date.
19. **Can I defer before I am matriculated?** No: "Deferment is asked for once you are matriculated".
20. **I was allocated a hostel bed but did not pay in time.** The hold lapsed (72 hours by default) and the bed passed to the next name. Ask the housing desk to seat you by hand in a free bed if any remains.
21. **Can anyone check my receipt / course form / certificate is real?** Yes. Each carries a QR or a code and a public page (`/verify/…`) that reads the University's record. The deferment letter's link and the PG offer letter's QR are exceptions — they cannot be verified online yet; the identity card has no verification page.
22. **As a lecturer, why is there no sheet for my course?** Either the HOD has not allocated you, or the Academic Office has not opened the examination session. Both are needed.
23. **I saved a wrong mark. How do I change it?** You cannot on your own: submit and ask the Examinations Officer or HOD to return the sheet with a reason; then amend the row with a reason.
24. **Where do I find my payslip?** Me → Leave & Payslip; approved or paid runs only, on screen (no PDF).
25. **What is the ICT ticket number format and can I track it without signing in?** `TICK-YYYY-NNNNN`; yes, at `/track` with the number and the email on the ticket.

## 11 Quick starts

### 11.1 New student — first ten things

1. Sign in at `/login` with your admission number and your applicant password (or the first password the Registry gave you, then choose your own).
2. Open **Profile** and add a personal email and phone — resets and notices go there.
3. Read the **Dashboard** fee-gate note; open **School Fees — Pay First**.
4. Generate a reference for the first semester (or the full session) and pay by card/USSD, PayDirect or at a bank quoting the reference.
5. When "Your payment is confirmed" arrives, download the **Receipt**.
6. Open **Course Registration**, tick your electives until the meter reads *Valid*, **Save the draft**, **Submit for approval**.
7. Check back for "Approved on …" (no email comes); download the **Course form**.
8. Wait for "Your matriculation number" by email/SMS; from then on sign in with it.
9. Visit **Identity Card** once the Library has issued your card; download the printable copy.
10. Bookmark **Notifications**, **Results** and **Help & Requests / ICT Support Tickets** for the session.

### 11.2 New lecturer — first ten things

1. Sign in with your staff number and the first password (`P<PNO>` if you were loaded from the nominal roll); choose your own password on **Your password**.
2. Check **Signed in as** shows *Lecturer* (switch if you also hold HOD or another office).
3. Open **My Profile** and upload a photograph, your contact details and your research lists.
4. Open **My Courses & Timetable**; if empty, ask your HOD to allocate your courses on Teaching Allocation.
5. On **Registered Students** set your **Timetable slots** and start recording **Attendance**.
6. Open **Course Spaces** and publish your first material; set assignments with their CA weight.
7. When the examination session opens, download the **Score Sheet** template from the dashboard.
8. Enter or upload marks on `/results/sheets/{id}`; give every candidate a mark or an outcome; **Submit and attest**.
9. Hold any script from a candidate not on the roll under **Scripts from candidates not on the roll**.
10. Track the sheet's stage on the dashboard; answer any **Result queries** on your courses within the department's desk.

### 11.3 New applicant — first ten things (undergraduate)

1. Open `/apply`, type your JAMB registration number and wait for "Found on the UTME/Direct Entry list".
2. Give an email you read, a Nigerian phone number and a password of eight characters or more; **Continue**.
3. On **Application Fee**, generate the reference and pay; wait for "Payment confirmed".
4. On **Application Form**, enter your next of kin, check the biodata and O'Level results JAMB sent, tick the declaration and **Submit application**.
5. Download the application form PDF for your records.
6. Watch **Screening Slip**; when the schedule is published, download the slip and note the reporting time, centre, room and seat.
7. Sit the examination with the slip and an identification document.
8. Read **Screening Result** when released, then **Admission Status** when the decision is released.
9. If offered: on **Accept Your Offer** sign the undertaking and pay the acceptance fee; print the offer letter.
10. Present your six originals at the Registry for clearance; when your admission number appears on **Matriculation**, sign in as a student and follow §11.1.

(A postgraduate applicant: apply at `/pg/apply`, pay the application fee, complete academic record, referees, documents and passport, pay the checking fee to read the decision, pay the acceptance fee to accept, then sign in as a student with the admission number.)
