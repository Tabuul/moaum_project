How the money actually moves, which is what this models: · NELFUND pays INSTITUTIONAL CHARGES directly to the University, in a bulk remittance covering many students at once. · NELFUND pays UPKEEP directly into the student's own bank account. It never passes through the University, and it does not appear in this wallet as spendable value — showing it there would teach students to expect cash at the Bursary. So the wallet is not a bank account the student controls. It is a ledger of institutional money held for them, which they apply to their own invoices.
